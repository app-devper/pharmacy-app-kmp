# Shared role policy

Roles and permissions must have one agreed meaning across the access service, pharmacy API, and KMP navigation. A client-visible route cannot imply permission that the backend denies. MANAGER may count and adjust stock, manage lots and suppliers, receive goods, edit customer profiles, and print labels. MANAGER may not change drug identity or selling prices, void a whole bill, administer KY, mutate users or store configuration, or access financial reports; read-only user listing remains allowed by the identity service. MANAGER may view KY exception status; only ADMIN or SUPER may resolve it, subject to validation against the applicable requirements. USER may return items against an existing bill when the reason and actor are recorded; voiding a whole bill remains ADMIN+. The current `summary`, `dashboard`, `daily`, `monthly`, and `top-drugs` report routes belong to ADMIN+ because they include financial or customer-identifying data; `slow-drugs` belongs to MANAGER+ as an operational stock view. KMP currently exposes MANAGER in administrative navigation while the pharmacy API rejects that role on administrative endpoints, and the pharmacy API exposes these reports to USER. Align route permissions and navigation before treating the policy as supported.
