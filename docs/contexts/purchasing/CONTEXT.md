# Purchasing

Purchasing is the context for ordering goods from suppliers and confirming their receipt.

## Language

**Purchase order**:
A record of goods ordered from an identified supplier, retaining the supplier name as it appeared when ordered.
_Avoid_: Stock lot, KY9 entry

**Supplier**:
An identifiable party from whom the pharmacy orders goods. A name alone is not its identity.
_Avoid_: Supplier name
