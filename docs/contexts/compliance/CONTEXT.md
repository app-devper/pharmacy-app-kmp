# Compliance

Compliance is the context for pharmacy regulatory records, including KY forms.

## Language

**KY9 entry**:
A regulatory purchase record associated with received goods. It is distinct from the purchase order and the stock lot.
_Avoid_: Purchase order, stock lot

**KY capture exception**:
A sale for which the cashier did not capture the expected KY information at checkout. The reason, actor, and time are retained until an authorized reviewer resolves it; it is not a completed KY record.
_Avoid_: Completed KY record
